# XPNSE privacy policy

Effective: 22 November 2026

XPNSE is an expense tracker for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data.

## What we collect
Nothing. XPNSE has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. We, the developer, never receive your expenses, messages or any other data.

## Where your data is stored
Everything you enter or import (expenses, budgets, trips, categories, settings) is stored in a database on your iPhone. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your iPhone, that database is included in those backups, under your control.

## Bank SMS
iOS does not let apps read your messages. If you choose to set up SMS capture, you create an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app that passes matching messages to XPNSE. XPNSE saves the message text on your iPhone so you can confirm it as an expense. Messages are not sent anywhere.

## Splitwise (optional)
If you connect Splitwise, XPNSE stores your Splitwise API key in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone. The app uses it to talk directly to Splitwise (secure.splitwise.com) to read your expenses, friends and groups, and to add expenses you choose to share. That data goes only between your iPhone and Splitwise and is covered by Splitwise's own privacy policy. You can remove the key at any time in Settings.

## Notifications
XPNSE may ask permission to show local notifications. They are scheduled on your iPhone; no notification data leaves the device.

## Backups and exports
When you make a backup (Settings > Back up now) or export a CSV, the file goes wherever you choose in the iOS share sheet, such as Files or iCloud Drive. XPNSE does not send it anywhere on its own. Backups contain your expenses, trips, budgets, categories, merchant names and appearance settings, but never your Splitwise key. Keep backup files somewhere private.

## Deleting your data
Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone. To remove the Splitwise key only, use Settings > Setup > Splitwise > Remove Splitwise key.

## Children
XPNSE is not directed at children under 13.
